Our Story

North Star Bakery started with a little bit of flour, a whole lot of butter, and a love for making life's sweetest moments feel even more special.

What began as late night baking in a tiny home kitchen quickly became something bigger. Cakes for birthdays turned into cupcakes for celebrations, cookies for friends became boxes for strangers, and before long, our kitchen simply couldn't keep up.

That's where North Star Bakery was born

Our name was inspired by the idea that no matter where life takes you, there is always something that brings you home. For us, that's the smell of something sweet in the oven, frosting covered countertops, and gathering around the table with the people you love most.

Today, North Star Bakery creates beautiful, handcrafted cakes, cookies, pastries, and sweet treats made to turn everyday moments into something worth celebrating. Everything we make is bakes with a little nostalgia, a touch of whimsy, and plenty of love.

Because we believe the best memories usually start with good people, a little celebration... and something sweet.

Made With the Good Stuff

We believe something made with love should start with ingredients chosen with just as much care.

At North Star Bakery, we keep things as simple: quality ingredients, thoughtful sourcing, and absolutely no cutting corners. We choose locally sourced ingredients whenever possible, work with small and independent suppliers, and select seasonal flavors when they're at their very best.

From farm-fresh eggs and real butter to rich chocolate, pure vanilla, and fresh seasonal fruit, every ingredient has a purpose.

Because beautiful desserts are lovely... but they should taste even better than they look.

Meet Emma

Owner, Baker & Professional Taste Tester

Hi, I'm Emma -- the flour covered hands (and slightly over-caffeinated heart) behind North Star Bakery

I've loved baking for as long as I can remember. Some of my favorite childhood memories happened in the kitchen -- standing on a chair beside my grandmother, sneaking bites of cookiedough, and learning that recipes made with a little extra love somehow always taste better.

Years later, baking became my creative escape. I started making cakes and treats for family and friends from my tiny home kitchen, usually with music playing, flour everywhere, amd more ideas than counter space.

One order became two. Two became twenty. And eventually, the little dream I couldn't stop thinking about became North Star Bakery.

Today, I get to spend my days creating desserts for birthdays, weddings, Tuesday pick-me-ups, and all the little moments in between.

When I'm not behind the mixer, you'll probably find me wandering a farmers market, drinking iced vanilla latte, rearranging flowers I definitely didn't need to buy, or dreaming up our next seasonal flavor.

I'm so happy you're here.

Stay Sweet, Emma
